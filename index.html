Key Hurdles

Critical Industry Friction

Professional hair salons, clinics, and medical institutions face heavy structural hurdles with current foreign offerings.

Extreme Weather Meltdown

Most global premium hair bonding products are formulated for Western, non-humid conditions. In India's high temperature and intense humidity, foreign bonds lose performance rapidly, melting to sticky chemical residues on patient scalps.

Exorbitant Cost Structure

Imported hair glues suffer from severe transport, custom, and distributor markup costs, causing prices to swell over 300%. Salons and clinics are forced to operate on slim margins while charging end-users premium rates.

Skin Damage & Unregulated Sourcing

Uncertified counterfeit or low-grade adhesive alternatives flooded into India can cause serious chemical burns, skin scaling, dermatitis, and long-term hair follicle destruction. Domestic certified options are virtually non-existent.

Product Development Grid

Our chemical research focuses on key dermatological safety rules.

1

Sweat-pH Adaption

Custom-tailored polymers designed not to react with typical scalp acidity levels or sebum secretions.

2

Allergen Protection

Complete absence of harmful raw ingredients and industrial solvents like formaldehyde and toluene.

3

Porosity Control

Micro-gaps built into the adhesive mesh allow air and sweat vapor to exit safely, keeping skin healthy.

4

Residue-Free Clean

Easily released with organic eco-solvents, leaving no sticky polymers attached to delicate hair grafts.

Our Progress

Historical Path & Waypoints

Where we stand today on our path to full commercial market scale.

September 2025 - Company Incorporation

Formally incorporated and registered Madheads Hair Chemical Pvt Ltd in Greater Noida, establishing legal and patent architectures for domestic medical-grade adhesives.

October 2025 - SRIIR Partnership

Partnered with the Shriram Institute for Industrial Research (SRIIR) to gain deep industrial validation, specialized chemical apparatus accessibility, and strict laboratory testing protocols.

December 2025 - DPIIT Certification

Received official certification from the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT), boosting startup benefits and government recognition.

Initiated the first localized adhesive glue trials on patient-simulated systems at Madheads Hair Studio to map sweat resistance, comfort profiles, and scalp adhesion hold times.

April 2026 - Upgraded 4th Sample Testing

Refined formula parameters to introduce our 4th generation sample with improved cohesive strength, humidity resistance, and skin protection matrices, currently under rigorous real-world evaluation.

May 2026 - Funding & Capital Expansion

Actively engaged in applying for Indian Government research grants and sourcing strategic private equity partners to accelerate regulatory licensing and pilot factory setups.
